YerasAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version: YerasAI collects no personal data. Your chats and settings stay on your phone. There is no account, no cloud backend, no analytics, and no ads.

YerasAI (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an offline AI chat app for Android developed by Addiss Lab. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. No account, no analytics, no ads

YerasAI has no sign-up, no login, no user profile, and no cloud backend. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. We do not receive your conversations, device identifiers, location, contacts, or usage statistics.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only in its private app storage on your device:

  • chat conversations and messages you create;
  • app settings (theme, temperature, context length, onboarding state);
  • downloaded model files and download metadata.

This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the app. You can delete individual conversations in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. Android cloud backup is disabled for YerasAI, so this data is not copied to Google’s backup service.

3. Model downloads

To chat, you download an open-source model file. The app requests that file directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over HTTPS. As with any web request, Hugging Face may receive technical information such as your IP address and user agent as part of serving the download. That request goes to Hugging Face, not to us, and is governed by Hugging Face’s own privacy policy. The downloaded model stays on your device.

After a model is installed, the app works without a network connection. The app requests only the INTERNET Android permission, and uses it solely for model downloads.

4. AI-generated content

Responses are generated locally by the model on your device. Your prompts and the generated text are not sent to us or to the model’s original publisher. The app does not train, fine-tune, or upload anything based on your conversations.

5. Children

YerasAI is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

Your chats and settings live in the app’s sandboxed private storage, protected by the Android operating system. Network requests for model downloads use HTTPS. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but the app is designed to keep your data on your device rather than in a cloud database.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes these practices (for example analytics or optional cloud sync), this policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that version is released. The “Last updated” date at the top will change.
